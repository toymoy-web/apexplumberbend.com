Health and safety

Handling a sewage spill safely

While you wait for the plumber

  • 1Wear rubber gloves and boots if you must enter the area, and wash well afterward.
  • 2Keep food, dishes and children away from the contaminated floor.
  • 3Open windows if the weather allows, to ventilate the smell.
  • 4Do not run the dishwasher or washing machine, since they push more water into the blocked line.
  • 5Do not use household bleach mixed with other cleaners, and avoid touching electrical outlets in wet areas.
  • 6If anyone feels ill, contact a doctor. We do not give medical advice.

If neighbors on your street are also affected, the problem may be in the public main, not your lateral. A quick call to the sewer utility is worth making.

Who is responsible

Your side of the line and the city's side

When sewage backs up, the first question is where the blockage sits. A plumber can answer that with a camera or by running a cable from the cleanout. If the obstruction is in the city main itself, it is the city's to clear, and the sewer utility should be notified. A blockage anywhere in the service between the main and your house is the owner's, according to the City of Bend's standards.

If it is on your side, the plumber clears the line and tells you what caused it. Roots and a collapsed joint need further work after the emergency is over, such as sewer lateral repair, and a camera inspection records the condition.

Septic systems have a different path. A backup in a home on septic may mean a full tank or a failed drain field, which is a separate licensed trade. The plumber can clear the house drain, and a septic professional handles the tank.

After the water stops

Cleanup and insurance

Sewage in a building is contaminated water, and cleanup follows specific health practices. Plumbing fixes the cause. Drying and sanitizing floors, walls and contents is handled by structural drying and restoration, which is a separate service and a separate dispatch.

For insurance, take photos before anything is moved. Many standard policies exclude sewer backup unless a backup endorsement was added, so ask early. Save the plumber's invoice and the camera video, since both document the cause.

Prevention

Lower the odds of a repeat

Habits and checks that help

  • 1Keep grease, wipes, paper towels and feminine products out of drains and toilets.
  • 2Have the main line cleared and scoped if the toilets gurgle after laundry.
  • 3Cut back or remove trees and shrubs that grow close to the lateral.
  • 4Keep the cleanout uncovered, labeled and capped.
  • 5Ask your insurer about a sewer backup endorsement before you need it.
  • 6Save camera footage from any past inspection, so a future plumber can compare.
